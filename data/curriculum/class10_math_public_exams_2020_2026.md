## 3. High-Frequency Authentic Public Exam Questions (2020 – 2026)

### Part I: Selected MCQs
1. If $n(A \times B) = 6$ and $A = \{1, 3\}$, then $n(B)$ is:
   - A) 1
   - B) 2
   - C) 3
   - D) 6
   *(Answer: C | Unit 1: Relations and Functions)*

2. The LCM of $6x^2y, 9x^2yz, 12x^2y^2$ is:
   - A) $36x^2y^2z$
   - B) $48xy$
   - C) $18x^2y$
   - D) $12x^2y^2z$
   *(Answer: A | Unit 3: Algebra)*

3. If the ratio of the terminal points of a line segment is in the ratio $1:2$, the section formula divides the line internally as:
   - A) $(\frac{mx_2+nx_1}{m+n}, \frac{my_2+ny_1}{m+n})$
   - B) $(\frac{x_1+x_2}{2}, \frac{y_1+y_2}{2})$
   - C) $(\frac{mx_2-nx_1}{m-n}, \frac{my_2-ny_1}{m-n})$
   - D) None of the above
   *(Answer: A | Unit 5: Coordinate Geometry)*

### Part II: Two-Mark Essential Questions
1. **Relations:** Let $A = \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$ and $B = \{2, 5, 8, 11, 14\}$ be two sets. Let $f: A \to B$ be given by $f(x) = 3x - 1$. Represent this function by: (i) arrow diagram, (ii) a table.
2. **Euclid's Lemma:** Find the Highest Common Factor (HCF) of 396, 504, 636 using Euclid’s Division Algorithm.
3. **Arithmetic Progression:** Find the 19th term of an A.P. $-11, -15, -19, \dots$
4. **Algebraic Matrix:** If $A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 3 \end{pmatrix}, B = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0 \\ 1 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$, find $AB$ and verify if $AB = BA$.
5. **Geometry:** State and formulate Thales Theorem (Basic Proportionality Theorem).
6. **Compulsory Problem (Q.No 28):** A cylindrical vessel of diameter 14 cm and height 20 cm is filled with water. Find the curved surface area (CSA) of the cylinder. ($\pi = 22/7$).

### Part III: Five-Mark Analytical Questions
1. **Arithmetic Series:** Find the sum of all natural numbers between 300 and 600 which are divisible by 7.
2. **Function Composition:** If $f(x) = 2x + 3, g(x) = 1 - 2x$ and $h(x) = 3x$. Prove that $f \circ (g \circ h) = (f \circ g) \circ h$.
3. **Quadratic Equation Solving:** Solve $\frac{1}{x-1} + \frac{2}{x-2} = \frac{8}{x}$ using the quadratic formula.
4. **Pythagoras Theorem:** State and prove Pythagoras Theorem using similarity of triangles.
5. **Statistics:** Find the coefficient of variation (C.V.) of the following data: $24, 26, 33, 37, 29, 31$.
6. **Compulsory Problem (Q.No 42):** From the top of a tower 50 m high, the angles of depression of the top and bottom of a tree are observed to be $30^\circ$ and $45^\circ$ respectively. Find the height of the tree. ($\sqrt{3} = 1.732$).
