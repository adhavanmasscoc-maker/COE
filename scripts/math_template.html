DGE Examination Blueprint & Chapter Weightage

Official Tamil Nadu SCERT Class 12 Mathematics Weightage Distribution:

  • Matrices & Determinants (Unit 1): 10–12 Marks (1 MCQ, 1 2-Mark, 1 5-Mark)
  • Complex Numbers (Unit 2): 8–10 Marks (1 MCQ, 1 3-Mark, 1 5-Mark)
  • Theory of Equations (Unit 3): 5–7 Marks (1 MCQ, 1 2-Mark or 3-Mark)
  • Analytical Geometry-II (Unit 5): 12–15 Marks (2 MCQs, 1 3-Mark, 1 5-Mark)
  • Vector Algebra (Unit 6): 12–15 Marks (2 MCQs, 1 2-Mark, 1 5-Mark)
  • Differential Calculus (Unit 7): 15–18 Marks (2 MCQs, 1 3-Mark, 1 5-Mark)
  • Integral Calculus (Unit 8 & 9): 12–15 Marks (2 MCQs, 1 2-Mark, 1 5-Mark)
  • Differential Equations (Unit 10): 10–12 Marks (1 MCQ, 1 3-Mark, 1 5-Mark)
  • Probability Distributions (Unit 11): 8–10 Marks (1 MCQ, 1 2-Mark, 1 5-Mark)
  • Discrete Mathematics (Unit 12): 8–10 Marks (1 MCQ, 1 2-Mark, 1 5-Mark)

Note: Q30 in Part II and Q40 in Part III are strictly Compulsory questions under DGE regulations.
