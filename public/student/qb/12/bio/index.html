DGE Examination Blueprint & Weightage — Biology

Official Tamil Nadu SCERT Class 12 Biology Scheme of Valuation (Theory: 70 Marks • Bio-Botany: 35 + Bio-Zoology: 35):

  • Bio-Botany (35 Marks): Reproduction in Plants (8M), Genetics & Molecular Biology (10M), Biotechnology (8M), Plant Ecology (9M).
  • Bio-Zoology (35 Marks): Human Reproduction & Health (10M), Genetics & Evolution (10M), Immunology & Human Diseases (8M), Applied Zoology (7M).
  • Compulsory Questions: Q24 in Part II (2 Marks) and Q33 in Part III (3 Marks labeled diagram) are compulsory.
