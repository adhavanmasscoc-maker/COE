DGE Examination Blueprint & Weightage — English

Official Tamil Nadu SCERT Class 12 General English Scheme of Evaluation (Theory: 90 Marks, 47 Questions):

  • Part I (Q1–20): 1-Mark Objectives (20 Marks): Synonyms (Q1-3), Antonyms (Q4-6), Idioms, Phrasal Verbs, Compound Words, Affixes, Abbreviations, Question Tags, Prepositions.
  • Part II Section 1 (Poetry Appreciation): Answer any 4 out of 6 (4 × 2 = 8 Marks)
  • Part II Section 2 (Grammar Transformation): Answer any 3 out of 4 (3 × 2 = 6 Marks) — Voice, Direct/Indirect, Conditionals, Simple/Compound/Complex.
  • Part III Section 1 (Explain with Reference to Context): Answer any 2 out of 3 (2 × 3 = 6 Marks)
  • Part III Section 2 (Prose Short Answers): Answer any 2 out of 3 (2 × 3 = 6 Marks)
  • Part III Section 3 (Study Skills): Answer any 3 out of 4 (3 × 3 = 9 Marks) — Dialogue, Notice, Advertisement, Non-verbal charts.
  • Part IV (Q41–47 Either/Or Long Answers, 35 Marks): Prose & Poetry Paragraphs, Letter Writing, Summary Writing / Note-Making, Comprehension.
