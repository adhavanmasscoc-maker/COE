DGE Examination Blueprint & Weightage — Computer Science

Official Tamil Nadu SCERT Class 12 Computer Science Scheme of Valuation (Theory: 70 Marks, 38 Questions):

  • Unit 1: Problem Solving & Functions: 10 Marks (2 MCQs, 1 2-Mark, 1 3-Mark, 1 5-Mark Either/Or)
  • Unit 2: Core Python & Control Structures: 8 Marks (2 MCQs, 1 2-Mark, 1 5-Mark Choice)
  • Unit 3: Modularity, Functions & Strings: 10 Marks (2 MCQs, 1 3-Mark, 1 5-Mark Choice)
  • Unit 4: Lists, Tuples, Sets & Dictionaries: 12 Marks (2 MCQs, 1 2-Mark, 1 3-Mark, 1 5-Mark Choice)
  • Unit 5: Classes and Objects (OOP): 10 Marks (2 MCQs, 1 2-Mark, 1 5-Mark)
  • Unit 6 & 7: Database Concepts & SQL: 15 Marks (2 MCQs, 1 2-Mark, 1 3-Mark, 1 5-Mark Choice)
  • Unit 8: Python & CSV: 8 Marks (1 MCQ, 1 2-Mark, 1 5-Mark Choice)
  • Unit 9: Python with C++ (MinGW): 5 Marks (1 MCQ, 1 2-Mark)
  • Unit 10: Data Visualization (Matplotlib): 6 Marks (1 MCQ, 1 2-Mark, 1 3-Mark)

Compulsory Questions: Q24 in Part II (Python Code Evaluation) and Q33 in Part III (SQL Table Definition/Query) are strictly compulsory.
